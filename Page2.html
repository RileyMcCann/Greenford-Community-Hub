Football Sessions

Join our weekly football sessions! Improve your passing, shooting and teamwork skills through friendly matches.

Schedule: Monday & Wednesday

Basketball Sessions

Join our exciting basketball sessions and develop your shooting, dribbling and passing skills! Take part in friendly matches, improve your teamwork and enjoy playing alongside other basketball enthusiasts.

Schedule: Tuesday & Thursday

Age Group: 12-18 Years

Price: £4 Per Session

Badminton Club

Get involved in our weekly badminton sessions! Improve your coordination, reaction time and racket skills while participating in friendly singles and doubles matches. All skill levels are welcome!

Schedule: Tuesday & Thursday

Age Group: 10-17 Years

Price: £5 Per Session

Cricket Club

Join our community cricket club and develop your batting, bowling and fielding skills! Participate in training exercises and friendly matches while building teamwork, confidence and sporting ability.

Schedule: Wednesday & Saturday

Age Group: 12-18 Years

Price: £4 Per Session

Athletics and Running Club

Improve your fitness, speed and endurance through our athletics sessions! Take part in running challenges, sprinting exercises and endurance training in a supportive and encouraging environment.

Schedule: Monday & Thursday

Age Group: 14+ Years

Price: £2 Per Session

Volleyball Sessions

Join our friendly volleyball sessions and learn essential skills such as serving, setting and passing! Work alongside teammates, improve your coordination and take part in exciting team matches.

Schedule: Friday & Saturday

Age Group: 13-18 Years

Price: £3 Per Session

Tennis Sessions

Learn the fundamentals of tennis or improve your existing abilities with our community tennis sessions! practice serving, returning and positioning while enjoying friendly games and competitions.

Schedule: Tuesday & Saturday

Age Group: 10-17 Years

Price: £5 Per Session

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to book in advance?

We recommend booking in advance as spaces are limited.

Is Equipment Provided?

Most of our activities provide the necessary equipment. However, participants are welcome to bring their own. We recommend wearing suitable sports clothing and footwear.

Are There Any Age Restrictions?

Yes, some activities have age restrictions to ensure the safety and enjoyment of all participants. You can find the appropriate age group listed alongside each activity.
